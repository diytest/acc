QUESTION: is an acceleration of 225g achievable in a washing machine during the spin cycle?
The measured value is close to the calculated one:
drum diameter 45 cm, radius r = 22.5 cm or 0.225 m;
the board with the MEMS accelerometer is placed inside a white plastic enclosure,
the MEMS accelerometer itself is located at a height of 7.6 mm from the base of the enclosure;
a gap of about 0.7 mm forms between the center of the enclosure base and the drum (circumference);
therefore for the MEMS accelerometer the rotation radius is r = 0.225 - 0.0076 - 0.0007= 0.2167 m;
rotation speed 1000 rpm or rotation frequency F=1000/60s=16.67 Hz;
angular velocity w = 2¶F = 6.28 * 16.67 = 104.69 Hz;
acceleration a = w² r;    a = 104.69² * 0.2167 = 2375.03 m/s²
or a = 2375.03 / 9.8 = 242.35 g  at 1000 rpm.
The measured rotation speed was 974 rpm instead of the expected 1000 rpm,
so the expected acceleration is a = 242.35 / (1000/974)² = 229.91 g  - the difference from the measured value of 225 g is 2%.

NOTE. The drum's rotation frequency is calculated from the MEMS accelerometer's sampling rate,
so the accuracy of the frequency measurement is determined by the quality of the MEMS accelerometer's clock oscillator, whose frequency is factory- trimmed with a tolerance of  ±2% at +25°C. Unfortunately, most MEMS accelerometers don't support external synchronization.

Bluetooth Accelerometers: parameters, software.

The Bluetooth 16g accelerometer and the Bluetooth 200g accelerometer are made on identical boards, differing only in their measured acceleration ranges. The remaining parameters and the software are identical.

VIDEO Bluetooth Accelerometer - standalone measurement with a real-time graph

Included software

lets you set the following measurement parameters:
- the measured acceleration range

of the Bluetooth 16g USB accelerometer     ±2g,   ±4g,     ±8g or ±16g, 

of the Bluetooth 200g USB accelerometer ±25g, ±50g, ±100g or ±200g,

- the number of accelerometer axes along which measurement is performed (using fewer than all 3 axes matters when plotting a graph from a large number of samples - plotting a graph from data arrays of several megabytes takes a noticeable amount of time; if only one axis, for example, gives informative results, the graph-plotting time can be cut by a factor of three),
- the accelerometer's sampling rate: 3200 Hz, 1600 Hz, 800 Hz, 400 Hz, 200Hz, 100Hz, 50Hz, 25 Hz, 12.5Hz or 6.25 Hz
- the measurement time / number of samples, limited only by the amount of free RAM on your computer.

The software lets you control the accelerometer, read out the measurement results, display them as acceleration graphs, and save the resulting data as files.
The computer application that works with the accelerometer provides convenient functions:

  1. viewing graphs,
  2. signal processing (smoothing, spectrum and trend calculation),
  3. searching for files by their comments,
  4. documenting results: 
  • saving graph data or fragments of it in txt text format or in csv format (for importing into MS Excel),
  • copying to the clipboard for subsequent pasting into documents, e.g. MS Word, PowerPoint,
    • graphs (in wmf format, image quality is not lost when compressing/stretching)
    • file comments, with file information (name, recording time) added automatically
    • spectrum calculation results (value and frequency of the maximum spectral component, all spectral components in text format) and trend results (the formula describing the trend line)
Additional feature of the Bluetooth Accelerometer application - at a sampling rate of no more than 100Hz, it's possible to view signal graphs in real time (oscilloscope mode), VIDEO Bluetooth Accelerometer  demonstrates exactly this mode.

The measurement parameters of the digital Bluetooth, USB, and RS-485 accelerometers are the same, and the user interface of these accelerometers' computer applications differs only slightly.
Therefore, the results presented for the USB and RS-485 accelerometers on the pages about accelerometer applications and accelerometer signal processing can also be obtained under the same conditions with Bluetooth accelerometers.
To get acquainted with the application and measurement results, you can download an archive with the demo application and help system for the USB accelerometer acc-usb . The demo application lets you open and process data files recorded by the accelerometers. An archive with some sample data files is located here: dataAcc-usb .
