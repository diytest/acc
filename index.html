1. Precision digital accelerometer

3 axes, USB or RS-485 interface, USB power,

software-switchable acceleration ranges ±2g/±4g/±8g,

wide dynamic range, low noise level, low drift.

The new precision MEMS accelerometer with low noise and low drift is designed for measuring small accelerations and has a wide dynamic range. The high sensitivity of the accelerometer allows it to be used for seismic measurements as well.

Precision accelerometer with RS-485 interface

  
The precision accelerometer RS-485 connects to the computer's USB, and it is capable of transmitting data over a cable several tens of meters long.
The kit includes the board of the precision accelerometer and an RS-485<>USB adapter board.
The precision accelerometer is powered via USB from the computer.
The board dimensions of the precision accelerometer are 29x29mm; it can be trimmed to a circle 29mm  in diameter to fit into the enclosure shown in the following photo:

Bluetooth accelerometer 16g  and accelerometer Bluetooth 200g are made on identical boards; they differ only in the range of measured accelerations. All other parameters and the software are identical.

Accelerometer Bluetooth can transmit measurement results, when used with the included adapter, to the computer over a distance of several tens of meters. 

The interface of the application differs little from the interface of the application for USB and RS-485 accelerometers; just as for these accelerometers, the graphs are plotted after the data transfer is complete.

Additional feature of the Bluetooth Accelerometer application - at a sampling rate of no more than 100Hz, measurement is possible with a real-time view of the signal graph (oscilloscope mode - the graph "runs" across the screen), video Bluetooth Accelerometer  demonstrates exactly this mode.

5. accelerometers with analog outputs

Accelerometers with analog outputs work with a cable (tens of meters) and a low-impedance load; ADCs for receiving and digitizing signals are also available. 

The most sensitive accelerometer measures accelerations in the range of ±1,5g.
Most accelerometers with analog outputs allow measuring accelerations in the frequency range from 0 to 1kHz.

High-frequency accelerometers
are able to measure acceleration in the frequency range from 0 to 30kHz.
They can be used to study impacts, as well as processes that require high time resolution.
It is possible to manufacture high-frequency accelerometers for impact loading with acceleration ranges of ±70g, ±250g or ±500g along with an ADC or separately.

6. System for measuring impacts in transport 

and

Measurement system for impact testing of packaging, tire treads, and helmets

The measurement system includes two high-frequency accelerometers, a 2-channel analog low-pass filter, an analog-to-digital converter with memory. 
Specific requirements for instruments for impact measurement - the use of high-frequency accelerometers with a large acceleration margin and filtering of the accelerometer signals analog (mandatory) low-pass filter with a steep frequency-response rolloff (no worse than -40dB/octave). The proposed systems meet ISO requirements for impact-test recording instruments.
